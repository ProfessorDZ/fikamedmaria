The woff2 files are the per-script subsets that Google Fonts serves (cyrillic,
latin, latin-ext), downloaded unmodified from fonts.gstatic.com. The @font-face
rules with their unicode-range are at the top of assets/styles.css. The
cyrillic-ext, greek and vietnamese subsets are left out because no page uses
those characters; add the matching file and rule if that changes.
